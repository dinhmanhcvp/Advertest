Future Roadmap: Advanced Egocentric Trajectories

1. Biomechanical & IMU Modeling

Transitioning from random 2D blur to physics-based trajectories driven by simulated IMU data (e.g., modeling 2Hz head-bobbing or chest-mount rotation patterns).

2. 3D Depth & Virtual Physics

Upgrading to Monocular Depth Estimation to project static images into a 2.5D space. Simulating a Virtual Camera with real-world optics (e.g., GoPro focal lengths) for true parallax and physically accurate Fisheye distortion.

3. Temporal Context Integration

Moving beyond frame-level YOLO by integrating optical flow and tracking (e.g., ByteTrack). Ensures bounding box persistence across highly degraded, consecutive frames where deterministic detection fails.
